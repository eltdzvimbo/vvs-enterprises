the label

Where music meets magic

VVS Enterprises is a music house built for artists who refuse to be boxed in. We protect the voice, expand the vision, and put culture-shifting sound on a global stage.

At VVS Records we champion visionary artists who push the limits of sound and style. From breakthrough newcomers to established names, every member of the roster brings a distinct voice — and we bring the platform, the live experience, and the community around it.

We believe in the power of collaboration. We partner with passionate artists and creators to craft timeless music that speaks to the soul and moves the culture forward.

Our values

Three principles guide every release, every show, and every partnership. They are the same words on our homepage because they are the work — not a tagline.

01
principle

Authenticity

Music that sounds like the artist, not the algorithm. We protect original voice, story, and identity at every stage of the process.

02
principle

Creative freedom

Room to experiment, evolve, and own the vision. We build space around artists so the work can stay fearless.

03
principle

Global community

Fans, collaborators, and stages that travel with you. From first stream to live night, we grow the culture around the catalog.
